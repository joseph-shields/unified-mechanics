# foundations/

Formal, physics-independent mathematical foundations underlying the motivation for the framework's single axiom.

**[`CANONICALIZATION.md`](CANONICALIZATION.md)** — a self-contained book in six Parts plus a governing Coda:

```
Part Zero   Representation Reduction   the method
Part One    Invariant Structure        the object
Part Two    Refinement Dynamics        the process
Part Three  Representation Extension   the recursion
Part Four   Realization Theory         geometry and scaling from the structure above
Part Five   From Closure to Cosmos     the application to Unified Mechanics, with falsifiers
Coda        The Law                    governance, master open-problem ledger, amendment register
```

Every claim is tagged **[T]**/proven, **[C]**/counterexample, *(working)*, or *(open)* at the point it's made — including one correction, found and fixed in the course of writing it, to an earlier draft's over-broad extremality claim (Part Four §4.4, Coda Amendment 001). The corrected result — φ as the minimum admissible self-refinement rate at exactly two roles, not universally — is what Part Five's audit uses when it revisits the axiom's motivation.

This folder is independent of the physics papers in `papers/` and `research/`: nothing here is required to check those derivations, and nothing in those derivations is assumed here. Part Five is where the two meet, in the open, with a falsifier stated for every load-bearing link.
