## Law 0 — The Axiom

> **φ² = φ + 1**

This is the only input. Everything else is derived. From this single self-referential algebraic condition, r = 1/(2φ) ≈ 0.30902 is fixed, and the three information channels follow:

```
W_L = (1 − r)² ≈ 47.75%    expanding
W_B = 2r(1 − r) ≈ 42.71%   boundary
W_M = r²        ≈  9.55%   contracting
W_L + W_B + W_M = 1         exactly
```

## Law 1 — The Floor

> **Every channel traversal costs exactly r³. Nothing costs less.**

The braiding floor ε_floor = r³ ≈ 2.95% is the irreducible information cost of one channel crossing. It is not a noise term or a measurement artifact. It is the minimum unit of physical process.

Sub-floor agreement on n ≥ 1 quantities **falsifies** UM as surely as a miss does. This is a two-sided bound, not a safety net.

## Law 2 — The Equation of Motion

> **Every recursive system obeys x_{n+1} = 1/(4x_n + 2).**

This map follows from φ_{n+1} = 1 + 1/φ_n by change of variable. Its unique physical fixed point is r. The system does not flow toward r because of boundary conditions — it flows because the equation of motion is the axiom acting as a restoring force. The polynomial 4x² + 2x − 1 = 0 is φ² = φ + 1 in x-coordinates.

## Law 3 — The Attractor

> **The fixed point r is stable. Its stability eigenvalue is −4r² = −4W_M = −1/φ².**

Convergence to r is oscillatory (negative eigenvalue) with contraction rate 1/φ² ≈ 0.382 per cycle. The rate is governed by the matter channel weight W_M. The boundary channel W_B > 0 is the necessary condition for convergence — without it the system cannot cross between expanding and contracting modes.

## Law 4 — The Arrow of Time

> **Time has a preferred direction. It is the direction in which r is stable.**

The axiom forces r = 1/(2φ) ≠ 1/2, which forces W_L ≠ W_M (specifically W_L/W_M = 5 exactly). The forward map is stable at r with eigenvalue −1/φ². The backward map is unstable at r with eigenvalue −φ². These are not equivalent. No past hypothesis is required — the asymmetry is in the algebra, not the initial conditions.

## Law 5 — Quantization

> **Action comes in integer multiples of r³. Nothing less is physical.**

Because channel traversals are discrete (N ∈ ℤ₊) and each costs r³, all action is A = n · r³. The energy spectrum near r is E_n = n · r³ · 2 ln φ — equally spaced, with zero-point energy r³ · ln φ. The quantum of action r³ plays the role of ℏ in UM natural units. Spin is the binary structure of the W_M channel (±r²).

*Derived in: Paper 13*

## Law 6 — The Observer

> **An observer is a W_M-stable structure exporting entropy through W_B. Observation is downstream of the channel partition, not prior to it.**

A system stable enough to record an outcome must have achieved local convergence to r — a W_M-dominated structure maintaining coherence by exporting entropy through W_B into W_L. The observer condition follows from the channel partition. The universe does not require an observer to have structure; the observer is a particular stable configuration of that structure.

*Derived in: Paper 09*

## What the Laws Do Not Say

These laws do not say that UM is complete. Open derivations include:

- The dimensional connection between r³ and the SI value of ℏ
- The gravitational kernel J(k²L²) from the axiom alone (Paper 10)
- The full Standard Model particle spectrum from W_M mode counting
- The arrow of time in quantum superpositions (decoherence under the UM map)

A law that covers everything and leaves nothing open is not a law — it is a tautology. The open derivations are listed because they are the next tests, not because they are failures.
